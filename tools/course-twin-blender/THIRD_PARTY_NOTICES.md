Original URLs, checksums, download dates and optimized outputs: assets.json. These are locally packaged exports; the browser does not call Poly Haven.

Mapped surface outlines, buildings, roads and parking © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright . The derived database is distributed separately in public/course-twins/common/context-v1. Each course JSON retains query URLs, source checksums and way IDs. Untagged road widths, building heights, roof shapes, facade details, parking bay layout and vehicle occupancy are inferred decorative representations.

Grass tufts (grass.py), tropical palms and their billboard (palm.py), and the unbranded compact car (car.py) are original procedural Blender geometry. No external asset licence applies to their original geometry. Existing aerial imagery and course assets retain their own attribution and licences; no rights change is implied.
