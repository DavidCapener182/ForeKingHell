## Candidate analysis

The audit compares club, ball speed, launch angle and club speed. It holds carry and spin out of matching to avoid selecting pairs merely because the quantities being calibrated agree. It returns all compatible candidates without forcing an assignment, assuming global order, fitting a bias, or presenting similarity as a probability.

Exploration windows are sensitivity checks, not validated device tolerances:

| Window  | Ball speed | Launch | Club speed |
| ------- | ---------: | -----: | ---------: |
| Narrow  |     ±2 mph |    ±1° |     ±2 mph |
| Working |     ±3 mph |    ±2° |     ±3 mph |
| Wide    |     ±5 mph |    ±3° |     ±5 mph |

Under the working window:

| Source                         | No candidate | One candidate | Multiple candidates |
| ------------------------------ | -----------: | ------------: | ------------------: |
| Rapsodo range, 6 shots         |            1 |             2 |                   3 |
| Rapsodo practice, 31 raw shots |           20 |             5 |                   6 |

One candidate is **not** a confirmed match. Several source shots compete for the same TrackMan shot. The audit treats the Rapsodo sessions independently; cross-session exclusivity has not been established either.

For example, Rapsodo range shot 1 (132.4 mph ball speed, 10.6° launch, 87.9 mph club speed, 205.7 yd carry) has three working-window candidates:

| TrackMan candidate | Ball speed | Launch | Club speed | Carry | Rapsodo minus TrackMan carry |
| ------------------ | ---------: | -----: | ---------: | ----: | ---------------------------: |
| driver_range_2     |      132.4 |   8.7° |       90.8 | 175.8 |                     +29.9 yd |
| driver_range_4     |      133.8 |   9.7° |       89.0 | 184.4 |                     +21.3 yd |
| driver_range_5     |      130.1 |  12.2° |       90.5 | 198.6 |                      +7.1 yd |

Those are alternative hypothetical pair differences, not measured device biases. Picking a convenient candidate would change the answer substantially. Wider windows create more ambiguity: five of the six range shots have multiple candidates. Under narrow windows, five have none. This does not prove the devices disagree or that they did not capture the same shot.

**Accepted pairs: 0. Bias, typical disagreement, 95% interval and calibration confidence: not estimated.** The fixture is a real-data ambiguity/rejection case, not a labelled matching ground truth or Calibration Session #1.
